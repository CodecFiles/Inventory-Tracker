04 · Device lifecycle

From the supplier box to a live facility

Every transition is an audit entry with who, when, before and after. Nothing is overwritten.

In preparationFirmware, profile, SIM, IP, test

ReservedOptional · held for a scheduled deployment
DeployedAt a facility as POC or Contract
Side paths, from any state: In transitUnder maintenanceReturnedDamagedRetired · POC → Contract keeps the same unit in place; only its deployment type changes.
